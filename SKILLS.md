## What AIG Force Does

AIG Force sources and places elite domain experts with companies that need deep, specialized knowledge. This is not a generalist hiring platform — every expert is vetted within their specific field. Companies get access to world-class talent. Experts get meaningful, high-value work.

## How It Works

### For Companies
1. **Submit a requirement** — describe the role, domain, and scope
2. **AIG Force vets and matches** — we shortlist domain experts from our network
3. **Review profiles** — companies review matched expert profiles
4. **Engage** — kick off the engagement with full AIG Force support

### For Experts
1. **Apply** — submit your domain, credentials, and availability
2. **Vetting** — AIG Force reviews your background and expertise
3. **Get matched** — receive relevant opportunities from vetted companies
4. **Work** — flexible engagements, remote-first
